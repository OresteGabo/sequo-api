# Presaved essentials

70 shared product families for the Vendor picker. Each shop keeps an offer pointing to its selected shared reference through `products.product_reference_id`. Brands, pack sizes, options, prices and photos belong to the seller offer. Canonical families such as Lait/Haricots are seeded once, not once per merchant.

English names/plurals and French accent variants are searchable aliases; they do not create additional product rows. The remaining clothing/electronics families and vendor-created references still appear in name suggestions; the Essentials picker shows only the reviewed starter list.
